Slice 0.2.7 is out. The newest iPhone joins the 3D editor, your existing iPhone recordings go straight onto it, and posing the device feels a lot smoother.

## iPhone 18 Pro Max

Open the **3D** tab and pick **iPhone 18 Pro Max** from the **Model** menu. It comes in **Burgundy**, **Silver**, **Deep Blue** and **Space Black**, and your recording plays on its display just like on the other phones.

## Dynamic Island, on or off

Phones now have a **Dynamic Island** switch. Leave it on for the real look, or turn it off to show your whole recording, with nothing covering the top of the screen.

## Bring your own iPhone recordings

Already have a screen recording from your iPhone or the Simulator? Drop it on the projects window to start a new project, or add it to a project you're editing. Slice recognises the iPhone it came from by its shape and puts it in that iPhone's frame, so it works in the flat mockup and on the 3D phone like a take you recorded through Slice. A small note above the preview shows the progress.

In the **3D** tab, **Add a recording…** does the same and puts the recording straight on the device it's from: an iPhone recording on the 3D iPhone, anything else on the Mac.

## Smoother posing

Turning, moving and resizing the device now glides instead of stepping with each mouse movement, and it never swings past where you left it. **Turn**, **Tilt** and **Spin** are now numbers you drag across, like in Keynote, or click to type an exact angle. The **Model** picker is now a simple menu, so there's more room for the device itself.

## Install or update

If you have 0.2.6, Slice will offer the update itself. Click **Update Now** on the card, or open **Settings › General** and click **Check for Updates**.

Otherwise, run this in Terminal:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://slice.rit3zh.dev/install.sh | bash
```

Or [download Slice.dmg](https://github.com/rit3zh/slice/releases/latest) and drag Slice to Applications. If macOS says it can't verify Slice, click **Done**, then go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. You only need to do this once.

Slice needs macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
